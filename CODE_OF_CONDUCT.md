# Lamda RED Code of Conduct

Lamda RED adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant]. Everyone
should be able to participate without harassment, regardless of who they are,
where they come from, or how much experience they have.

## Participation standards

Treat other people with respect. Give feedback about the work, listen to different
perspectives, and take responsibility when your actions affect others. Harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing
someone's private information without consent violate this policy.

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and
reviews, and when someone officially represents Lamda RED elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an incident on GitHub, open the menu on the relevant issue, pull request, or
comment and select **Report content**. Choose **Report to repository admins** if
GitHub offers that option. If it is unavailable, or if the report concerns a
maintainer, use the [GitHub abuse reporting process][github-reporting]. Keep
incident details out of public issues and comments.

Lamda RED maintainers are responsible for reviewing the reports they receive
fairly and promptly and protecting the reporter's privacy. They may moderate
content and apply the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines][enforcement],
from a private correction or warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending
on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
